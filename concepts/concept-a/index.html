Wood-fired cooking. Rooted in tradition. Timeless West.

An open-fire parrilla drives the menu: oak, mesquite, and iron. Ranch recipes carried into Nevada's high desert and served with a full bar, a river out front, and a neighborhood cantina spirit.

A family table,
moved West.

Rancho Cantina started as a family restaurant, and the recipes still come from that kitchen. In Reno they meet the Great Basin: Nevada beef, open fire, pine nuts and sagebrush country, and the buckaroo tradition of cooking over coals.

The rancho was never just land. It was the place where people gathered, work was respected, and strangers became friends. That is the whole idea.

Rancho by tradition. Reno by nature. Always by heart.
