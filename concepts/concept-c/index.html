Rancho noun a place where people gather. Where work is respected, meals are shared, and strangers become friends. Where the cooking happens over almond wood and mesquite, and the table sits on the river.

The vaquero

Spanish cattle, open range, and the riders who worked it. The first ranch cooking in the West was done over coals, on iron, outdoors.

The ranch table

Family recipes seasoned the Rancho way and kept for generations. Mex-Western cooking built around wood fire, bold flavors, great ingredients and the spirit of the west.

The buckaroo

Nevada ranch country, 1860s on. Great Basin cattle, buckaroo camps in the sagebrush, and a table built on beef, beans, and coals.

The rancho, today

Reno, on the Truckee. Same fire, same welcome. Rancho by tradition. Reno by nature. Always by heart.
